Engineering Notes
A ~3.0 mm trace on 1.6 mm FR-4 is a classic solution for 50 Ω — this width-to-height ratio (roughly 1.9:1) is right in the expected ballpark for standard FR-4.
This assumes an unclad (plain) surface microstrip with a solid ground plane beneath. If you plan to route this through a connector transition or bury it in a multilayer stack, the impedance will shift.
The 54.4 ps/cm delay gives you roughly 6.7 in/ns of propagation velocity (~66% of speed of light) — typical for FR-4.
