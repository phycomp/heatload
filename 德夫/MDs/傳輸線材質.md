Cryogenic Cables ==> Our cryogenic cables deliver precision, reliability, and performance at ultra-low temperatures. From coaxial lines for high-frequency signals to miniature shielded cables for compact systems, our designs ensure clean, dependable data transmission.
Versatile designs: single conductors or twisted pairs
Superior materials: brass or superconducting wire options
Low-noise shielding: optimized for sensitive measurements
Thermal Solutions => Managing heat at cryogenic temperatures is critical. Our thermal solutions are engineered to keep systems stable, efficient, and reliable, using multiple methods to control heat generation:
Copper heatsinks bonded to ribbon cable assemblies with thermal epoxy
Epoxy back shells for robust thermalisation
Connector flanges or braid straps tied to a heat stage ground plane
Materials => Every application is unique, which is why we offer a wide range of wire materials to suit your exact needs: Copper, Manganin, NbTi, CuNi, "Phosphor Bronze", "Stainless Steel", "Beryllium Copper", ALI30
