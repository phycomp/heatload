SMA connectors are threaded, semi-precision connectors (DC–18/26.5 GHz) ideal for secure, durable RF connections. SMP connectors are miniature, push-on/snap-on connectors offering higher frequency performance (up to 40 GHz), ideal for dense, board-to-board, or high-vibration applications. Both are 50-ohm, but SMP allows faster installation and blind-mate connections.

### SMA (Sub-Miniature version A) Connectors

- Interface: Threaded barrel (thread) for high security.
- Frequency: Typically DC–18 GHz, some precision models to 26.5 GHz.
- Best For: Long-term connections, high power, and general microwave systems, including Antennas and testing.
- Durability: High, commonly used in durable and MIL-STD applications.

### SMP (Sub-Miniature Push-on) Connectors

- Interface: Push-on, snap-on, or snap-in (blind-mate).
- Frequency: Excellent performance up to 40 GHz.
- Best For: Compact, high-density, board-to-board, and high-vibration scenarios (telecom, aerospace).
- Retention Types: Full detent, limited detent, and smooth bore.
- Size: SMP is significantly smaller than SMA.

### Installation: SMA is screwed; SMP is pushed in.

- Frequency: SMP supports higher frequencies than standard SMA.
- Application: SMA is better for robust external connections; SMP excels in tight modular packaging.
